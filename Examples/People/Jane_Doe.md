# Jane Doe

**Jane Doe** (born 1972) is a fictional surveyor and the inventor of the *Doe projection*. She is married to the cartographer [John Doe](John_Doe).

## Early life

Jane Doe was born in the inland town of Hollow Reach and spent her childhood measuring things that did not need measuring: the height of the church steeple, the width of the river in flood, the distance to the horizon.

## The Doe projection

The Doe projection is a map projection that preserves the reader's patience at the expense of area, shape and distance. It is used in every map in her husband's [atlas](John_Doe#the-atlas).

## Field work

1. Choose a coastline.
2. Walk it.
3. Write down everything.
   - Tides
   - Rocks
   - Unusually opinionated seabirds
4. Walk it again, in case it changed.
