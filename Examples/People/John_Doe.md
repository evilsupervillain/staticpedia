**John Quincy Doe** (born 3 March 1970) is a fictional cartographer known for meticulous maps of coastlines that do not exist. He and his wife, the surveyor [Jane Doe](Jane_Doe), are the sample subjects of the Static-Pedia manual.[^fiction]

## Early life

Doe grew up in the harbour town of Port Ellery, where his father kept the tide tables. He drew his first map, of the family garden, at the age of seven. It is still considered "ambitious in scale".[^garden]

## Career

### Surveying

After studying geodesy, Doe joined the Meridian Survey Office in 1991. There he met [Jane Doe](./Jane_Doe.md), whose [projection](Jane_Doe#the-doe-projection) he later used for all of his published work.

### The atlas

His best-known work, the *Atlas of Coasts That Never Were* (2004), collects 112 maps drawn to survey standards for places that exist only on paper. Reviewers praised its consistency. Every invented river flows downhill.
